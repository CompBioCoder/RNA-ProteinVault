> [!abstract] Abstract
> Functional RNA design is central to synthetic biology and RNA therapeutics. Recent AI advances leverage tertiary structure to interpret function and guide sequence generation, replacing traditional trial-and-error workflows. Experimental methods assisted by AI models continuously expand the structural data foundation and ultimately enable scalable and programmable RNA design.
