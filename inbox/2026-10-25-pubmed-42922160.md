**Deng-Jian-Yi Xu, Bin Yang, Wen-Chao Xu, Nan Yang, Sheng Xin, Ji-Hong Liu, De-Lin Ma, Xia-Ming Liu**  
PubMed · 2026-10-23 · Asian J Androl · [原文](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42922160/)

> [!abstract] Abstract
> RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) govern RNA splicing, stability, translation, and localization through RNA-recognition motifs, K-homology domains, and related structures, orchestrating spermatogonial stem cell (SSC) self-renewal, meiosis, and spermiogenesis. We present the stage-specific roles of key RBPs, such as deleted in azoospermia protein/deleted in azoospermia-like protein (DAZ/DAZL), nanos C2HC-type zinc finger 2 (NANOS2), P-element-induced wimpy testis protein (PIWI) family members, and tudor domain-containing 6 (TDRD6), and illustrate their molecular mechanisms in SSC maintenance, meiotic progression, and sperm morphogenesis. The review assesses RBP expression and modifications as diagnostic biomarkers for male infertility, evaluates RBP-targeted therapies, and notes translational obstacles. Emerging interdisciplinary approaches promise to decode spatiotemporal RBP networks and advance precision diagnosis and treatment of male reproductive disorders.
