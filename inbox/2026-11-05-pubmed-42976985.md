PubMed · 2026-11-03 · J Mol Biol · [原文](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42976985/)

> [!abstract] Abstract
> While cancer-associated SF3B1 K700E mutation altered production of circRNAs, including circATP9B, the underlying molecular mechanism remained unexplored. Here, we found that SF3B1 mutation enhanced interaction between SF3B1 and SFPQ, an RNA-binding protein involved in the formation of circRNA, and SFPQ bound to flanking introns of the back-splicing site of circATP9B. Deletion of the potential binding sites of SFPQ in the flanking introns decreased the production of circATP9B. Furthermore, enrichment analysis demonstrated that SFPQ binding sites were enriched in the flanking introns of the back-splicing sites of other dysregulated circRNAs associated with SF3B1 mutation. In addition, circATP9B bound to NPM1, resulting in the retention of NPM1 in cytoplasm, which suppressed p53-dependent apoptosis. In summary, we revealed the mechanism by which SF3B1 mutation promotes circATP9B biogenesis, and demonstrated that circATP9B mediates apoptosis through NPM1.
