**Hao Sun, Linyan Hu, Xuemei Peng, Wei Zhang, Jiajun Lin, Jingyi Huang, Cheng Zhang, Lin Huang et al.**  
PubMed · 2026-11-05 · Nucleic Acids Res · [原文](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42984443/)

> [!abstract] Abstract
> RNA chemical probing generates nucleotide-resolved profiles in vitro and in cells, while X-ray crystallographic or cryo-electron microscopy models provide high-resolution structural models but remain comparatively low throughput. Aligning these experiments from different modalities may provide insights into RNA structure formation and molecular functions. Here, we present Ribocentre-FoldBridge (https://foldbridge.ribocentre.org/), an AI-assisted, curator-reviewed database linking curated chemical-probing profiles with experimentally determined 3D structures. This database catalogues 12 types of RNAs, linking 256 929 probing profiles across five experimental categories to 5321 structure-linked cases. It interactively visualizes the probing profiles that align with RNA secondary and tertiary structures at nucleotide resolution. We demonstrate probe-specific reactivity patterns across paired, unpaired, and solvent-accessible structural contexts. The database aims to provide a reproducible evidence layer for examining RNA structure-function relationships across heterogeneous experiments, and thus facilitate the development of new RNA structure or function prediction programs.
