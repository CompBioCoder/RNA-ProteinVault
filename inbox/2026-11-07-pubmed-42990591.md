> [!abstract] Abstract
> • This manuscript introduced GEMORNA, a comprehensive platform leveraging deep generative AI to autonomously design highly optimized, full-length mRNA and circular RNA sequences. • This represents a significant paradigm shift from traditional, often manual and piecemeal, RNA optimization strategies. • GEMORNA-generated sequences achieve remarkable enhancements in protein expression, stability, and delivery efficiency, while simultaneously mitigating immunogenicity—a major hurdle in RNA therapeutics.
