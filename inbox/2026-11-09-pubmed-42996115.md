> [!abstract] Abstract
> To bridge the gap between rapid RNA sequence discovery and slow experimental characterization, we present the NuFold RNA Structure Database ( https://nufold.kiharalab.org/ ), a resource providing large-scale, precomputed full-atom RNA tertiary structures. By removing the computational barriers associated with running the method locally, this database makes high-accuracy RNA structure models readily accessible to the biological research community for a wide range of applications. All predicted structures and corresponding data are available at https://nufold.kiharalab.org/ . The source code for the NuFold program is available at https://github.com/kiharalab/NuFold . For more insight into resulting statistics gathered for this project, see https://nufold.kiharalab.org/statistics . For more insight into how to utilize the webserver itself, see https://nufold.kiharalab.org/faq and https://nufold.kiharalab.org/tutorial .
