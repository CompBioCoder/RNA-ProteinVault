PubMed · 2026-11-13 · Cell Rep · [原文](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43024504/)

> [!abstract] Abstract
> Under stresses, such as arsenite (AS), protein-RNA condensates form in the cytoplasm, named as stress granules (SGs). Although believed to be important, the characteristics and functions of RNAs, as well as the RNA-RNA interactomes in SGs, are still unclear. Here, we performed G3BP1 irCLASH-seq under AS- and AS+, consequently capturing the in vivo G3BP1-RNA and G3BP1-mediated RNA-RNA interactomes, and revealed that RNA features and RNA-RNA interactomes under AS- and AS+ are distinct. Two groups of RNA hubs regulating SG formation via intermolecular RNA-RNA interactions were identified. Strikingly, AS+-specific AU-rich (ARMs) and AS--specific GC-rich motifs (GRMs) were found positively and negatively correlated with SG enrichment of RNAs, while ARMs promote and GRMs inhibit SG assembly, even their insertion confers an RNA the regulatory function. Collectively, our findings provide insights into the crucial role of RNAs in SGs and a resource for the in vivo RNA-RNA interactomes of SGs.
