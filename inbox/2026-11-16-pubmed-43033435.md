> [!abstract] Abstract
> The role of RNA-binding proteins in metabolic gene regulation is unclear. Here, we uncover a hierarchical circuit where RNA-binding protein RBFOX2 establishes lipid gene programs and lipidome in embryonic hearts by governing transcription factors (TFs) using multi-transcriptomic and lipidomics approaches. We identified TF and lipid genes as RBFOX2 targets in the embryonic heart. TF SOX4 is a key target of RBFOX2 that drives lipid gene programs. RBFOX2 binds human SOX4 3' UTR and regulates its mRNA stability. SOX4 in turn initiates a lipid gene program by binding promoters of lipid enzymes/regulators. Restoration of SOX4 level, rescues lipid gene expression defects upon RBFOX2 loss. Strikingly, Rbfox2 loss in embryonic hearts modulates cardiac lipidome and leads to accumulation of lipid droplets. We reveal an RNA-mediated mechanism that establishes lipidome identity in the developing heart and may provide unique metabolic nodes vulnerable for pharmaceutic intervention of congenital heart disease in the future.
