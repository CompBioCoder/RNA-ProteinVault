PubMed · 2026-11-17 · J Struct Biol · [原文](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43044699/)

> [!abstract] Abstract
> A theophylline-binding RNA aptamer has been extensively studied as a model for RNA-ligand interactions. While previous structural analyses have provided insights into its binding mechanism, the precise nature of ligand recognition remains unclear. In this study, we present a crystal structure of a novel ligand-free conformation of the theophylline aptamer. Unlike previously reported ligand-free conformations within Fab-RNA complexes, the ligand-binding region adopts a well-organized structure in which the space occupied by theophylline in the ligand-bound state is instead filled by an RNA nucleotide. Structural evaluation with a residue-separation-based statistical potential revealed that the newly identified ligand-free conformation shows greater consistency with statistically preferred intramolecular atomic arrangements than the other ligand-bound and ligand-free conformations examined. These findings provide new insights into the conformational landscape underlying theophylline recognition.
