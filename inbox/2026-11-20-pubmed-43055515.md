> [!abstract] Abstract
> RNA G-quadruplexes (RG4s) are noncanonical four-stranded RNA structures that regulate post-transcriptional processes and are linked to multiple diseases, yet identifying their binding proteins remains experimentally challenging and computationally limited. Here, we present G4SAINT, a deep learning framework that predicts RG4-binding potential directly from protein sequence, structural, and domain-guided features. G4SAINT integrates residue-level embeddings from protein language models with graph neural networks and domain-guided attention to capture complementary signals across modalities. Across multiple benchmarks, G4SAINT outperforms existing approaches and accurately highlights regions associated with RG4 recognition. By leveraging multi-task learning across related binding prediction tasks, it enables scalable application to large, uncharacterized human proteomes. Overall, G4SAINT provides a robust and extensible approach for systematic discovery of RG4-binding proteins, offering new opportunities for understanding RNA-protein interactions and identifying potential therapeutic targets. Our code and datasets are available at: https://github.com/o-nwn-o/G4SAINT.
