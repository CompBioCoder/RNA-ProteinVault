> [!abstract] Abstract
> Structural comparison of nucleic acids, particularly RNA, is critical for understanding evolutionary and functional relationships beyond sequence similarity, yet efficient tools for large-scale 3D structure search and alignment remain scarce. We extend GTalign to support nucleic acid structures, enabling unified, high-performance alignment across macromolecules. Benchmarking on diverse nucleic acid datasets demonstrates that GTalign achieves higher alignment accuracy, as measured by TM-score, than the other benchmarked methods, while its faster configurations achieve substantially reduced runtimes at the expense of some sensitivity. GTalign thus provides a scalable solution for nucleic acid structure comparison and database search. https://github.com/minmarg/gtalign_alpha.
